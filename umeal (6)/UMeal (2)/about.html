About UMEAL

UMEAL is a digital canteen ordering system developed for the students and staff of UM Tagum College. It simplifies the traditional food ordering process by allowing users to browse meals, add them to cart, and place orders directly through the system.

The main objective of UMEAL is to reduce long queues, avoid miscommunication in orders, and improve canteen efficiency. The system provides students with real-time order status (Pending, Ready, Walk-in, Claimed, Cancelled) while giving staff a tool to manage and update orders instantly.

By digitizing the canteen service, UMEAL promotes convenience, transparency, and better time management inside the campus.
